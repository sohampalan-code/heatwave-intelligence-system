Climatological & Meteorological Data Sources

Our analytics engine integrates multiple validated observational feeds to provide dependable early-warning metrics.

1. Heatwave Early Warning Operational Workflow (Ordered List)

  1. Sensory Data Collection: Automated Weather Stations (AWS) transmit 15-minute telemetry.
  2. Satellite Data Synchronization: INSAT-3D thermal land-surface scans calibrated with regional baselines.
  3. Heat Index & Threshold Evaluation: Mathematical computation of apparent temperature and anomalies.
  4. Severity Classification: Algorithmic categorisation into Green, Yellow, Orange, or Red alerts.
  5. Emergency Multi-Channel Broadcast: Real-time dispatch of localized bulletins to stakeholders and citizens.

2. Primary Data Ingestion Sources (Unordered List)

  • AWS Ground Sensors: Precision thermistors, hygrometers, and pyranometers.
  • IMD Pune Synoptic Database: Official climatological normals, surface observatories, and Doppler radar maps.
  • ISRO Geostationary Satellite Feeds: INSAT-3D/3DR land surface temperature (LST) and NDVI telemetry.
  • Numerical Weather Prediction Models: GFS, ECMWF, and regional WRF atmospheric ensemble models.

3. Stakeholder Roles & Action Matrix (Nested List)

  • Disaster Management Authorities (NDMA / SDMA)
    • Declare municipal emergency protocols and cooling shelters.
    • Enact work hour restrictions for heavy outdoor construction labor.
    • Mobilize emergency water tankers to urban heat islands.
  • Public Health & Trauma Centers
    • Prepare dedicated heat-stroke emergency triage units and saline supplies.
    • Track hospital admissions for dehydration and heat exhaustion.
  • General Public & Vulnerable Communities
    • Subscribe to real-time SMS/WhatsApp warning bulletins.
    • Adopt hydration measures and restrict peak-hour outdoor exposure.

4. Meteorological Nomenclature & Abbreviations (Description List)

AWS
Automated Weather Station — An autonomous meteorological telemetry unit collecting temperature, humidity, and radiation data at high frequency.
IMD
India Meteorological Department — The principal national agency in charge of meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology.
HW
Heat Wave — A period of abnormally high temperatures, typically 4.5°C to 6.4°C above normal seasonal baselines.
SHW
Severe Heat Wave — An extreme meteorological state where maximum temperatures exceed 45°C or depart by > 6.4°C from climatological normal.
