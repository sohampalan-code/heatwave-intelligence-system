Standard Heatwave Protective Measures (Do's & Don'ts)

✓ Recommended Actions (Do's)

Personal Protection & Community Care:

  • Drink sufficient water and oral rehydration solution (ORS) frequently.
  • Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored cotton clothing.
  • Cover head with a wide-brim hat, umbrella, or wet cloth outdoors.
  • Keep domestic pets and livestock in shaded areas with ample water.

✗ Prohibited Actions (Don'ts)

High Risk Activities to Avoid:

  • Do not leave children or pets inside parked vehicles.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure during peak thermal window (12:00 PM - 3:30 PM).
  • Avoid caffeinated, sugary, or alcoholic drinks that accelerate dehydration.
  • Avoid strenuous heavy labor during the hottest hours of the day.

* Advisory Source: Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) & NDMA Guidelines.
