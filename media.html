Emergency First Aid Protocol for Severe Heat Exhaustion

  1. Immediately move the affected person to a cool, shaded, or air-conditioned area.
  2. Lay the person down and elevate their legs slightly.
  3. Loosen tight clothing and apply cool, wet towels or ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
  4. Provide small sips of cool water or electrolyte drink if the person is conscious.
  5. Dial emergency helpline 108 immediately if the person exhibits confusion, vomiting, or loss of consciousness.
