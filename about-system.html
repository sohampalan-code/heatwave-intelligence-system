About the Climate Intelligence Architecture

The Heatwave Intelligence System (HIS) is an AI-driven, IoT-enabled meteorological framework that bridges ground-level weather station observations with predictive climatic models.

"Timely and localized heatwave intelligence transforms reactive emergency relief into proactive community survival. Our integrated AWS sensor network and predictive thermal algorithms empower municipal corporations to save lives hours before peak heat waves strike."

— Dr. A. K. Srivastava, Chief Meteorologist & Disaster Management Officer, IMD Pune

🎯 System Objective & Scope

The primary objective is to minimize heatwave-induced mortality and morbidity through automated 15-minute microclimate data acquisition, AI-based heat index computations, and rapid multi-channel advisory dissemination to civil authorities and vulnerable citizen groups.

🛰️ Integrated Data Sources

Our ingestion pipeline merges ground-level telemetry from IoT Automated Weather Stations (AWS) with geostationary satellite radiometric feeds (INSAT-3D/3DR) and Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) global ensemble models.

👥 Key Stakeholders & Target Beneficiaries

Key stakeholders include National & State Disaster Management Authorities (NDMA/SDMA), Municipal Health & Trauma Care Centers, Public Transport Departments, and At-Risk Citizens (outdoor workers, senior citizens, and children).

1. IoT Automated Weather Stations (AWS)

Equipped with calibrated thermistors, capacitive hygrometers, pyranometers, and ultrasonic anemometers recording micro-climatic readings every 15 minutes.

2. Satellite Thermal Telemetry

Ingests multi-spectral land surface temperature (LST) and normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI) from geostationary meteorological satellites.

3. Predictive AI Modeling

Combines historical heatwave occurrences, atmospheric moisture vectors, and urban heat island (UHI) geometry to generate 7-day predictive heatwave models.

4. Multi-Channel Advisory Dispatch

Automated SMS bulletins, WhatsApp broadcasts, and web APIs alert municipal corporations, hospital trauma units, and schools in real-time.
